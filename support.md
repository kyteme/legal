## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I delete my account?

Send an email from the email address associated with your Kyte account to:

legal@kyte.me

Email subject: Accout Deletion

Email body must include:

Your registered email address
A clear statement requesting permanent deletion of your Kyte account and associated personal data

### Notifications are not working
Please ensure push notifications are enabled in your device settings.

### How does AI processing work?
Kyte uses automated systems to generate summaries and reminders from conversations. AI outputs are assistive only.
